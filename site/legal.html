Privacy notice

LATENT AURUM LTD is the controller of the personal data described here.

What we collect

This site has no accounts and no tracking. When you send the contact form, we receive your name, your email address, what your message is about, your company if you give it, and your message. The form passes them to EmailJS, which delivers them to us as an email. If JavaScript is switched off in your browser, the form opens your own email app instead, and nothing reaches us until you send that email. When you email us directly, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. in the United States. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account. GitHub handles those logs under its own privacy statement, which also says how long it keeps them. We do not receive them, and we do not use them to identify you.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your details to reply to you and to talk about working together. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering the people who write to us. When we are discussing an agreement with you, the basis is taking steps at your request before a contract. Hosting logs are kept by GitHub to keep the service secure.

Who sees it

The contact form is delivered by EmailJS, a service of EmailJS Pte. Ltd. in Singapore, which acts for us as a processor. Its servers are in the United States and run by Amazon Web Services. EmailJS describes how it handles data in its privacy policy. Your email is handled by our email provider, Google, through Google Workspace. We do not sell personal data. We do not pass it to advertisers. If a provider stores data outside the UK, it does so under safeguards recognised by UK data protection law. GitHub, for example, takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for 12 months after the last contact, and longer where they form part of a contract or where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data. You can ask us to correct it or delete it. You can object to how we use it, or ask us to limit it. Where it applies, you can ask for your data in a form you can take elsewhere.

Write to us at the email address above. We will reply within one month. We do not make decisions about you by automated means. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children
