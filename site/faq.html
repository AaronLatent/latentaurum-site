Questions.

Short answers to what people ask us most.

What is Latent Aurum?

Latent Aurum is a content studio in London that makes original films, series, shorts, animation and music. Its work is made by people and assisted by AI. It was founded in 2026 by Philip Knatchbull, Giovanni Damiani and Aaron Bhugobaun. The name means hidden gold in Latin.

Who founded Latent Aurum?

Philip Knatchbull, the chair, was CEO of Curzon for 17 years. Giovanni Damiani, the CCO, worked at Decca and Universal and co-founded Only Good People. Aaron Bhugobaun, the CEO and CTO, spent ten years delivering films for Marvel, Disney, Sony and Universal, and was AI and technical production manager at Moonbug Entertainment.

What does Latent Aurum make?

Original stories for every age: children's entertainment, anime, musicals and movies. It also writes and produces music and music videos with world-class songwriters and composers, and works with artists around the world.

How does Latent Aurum use AI?

As a tool, not the final product. Every project starts with people: the story, the script, the songs and the characters. AI helps the team move faster, and leading tastemakers direct the final cut. The studio builds its own system for this, called Latent OS.

How can I work with Latent Aurum?

Send a message through the contact form and choose what it is about: a query, a collaboration or an opportunity. Artists, writers, composers and partners are all welcome to get in touch.
